FAQ

We're here to answer your questions

Clear answers to the most common questions about Notifima.

Notifima is a WooCommerce plugin that lets customers subscribe to out-of-stock products and automatically notifies them by email when those products are restocked. It replaces the dead-end "Out of Stock" message with a smart waitlist form and a demand-tracking admin dashboard.
Install Notifima on your WordPress site. When a product goes out of stock, it automatically shows a subscription form where customers enter their email. When you restock the product, Notifima sends an automated email to all subscribers with a direct link to buy.
Yes. Notifima supports simple, variable and grouped WooCommerce products. Customers can subscribe to a specific variation, such as a particular size or colour, not just the parent product.
Yes. Guests subscribe with their email address, and registered customers can manage all their subscriptions from the My Account dashboard.
Notifima starts at $49/year for a single site licence. A 3-site Business licence costs $69/year and a 10-site Agency licence costs $99/year. All plans include Pro features, priority support and one year of updates. A 15-day money back guarantee applies to every purchase.
Yes. Notifima Pro includes double opt-in, which sends a confirmation email before adding the customer to a waitlist. This improves list quality, reduces spam complaints and helps with GDPR compliance.
Notifima supports GDPR compliance through double opt-in, one-click unsubscribe in every email, and a self-service page where customers can view and remove their subscriptions at any time. No subscriber data is shared with third parties unless you set it up.
Yes. Notifima Pro syncs waitlist subscribers to your Mailchimp audience automatically, so you can segment them by product and run campaigns without any manual export.
Yes. Notifima Pro lets you block specific email addresses or entire domains. You can also turn on Google reCAPTCHA to stop bots, and set custom error messages for rejected signups.
From the Notifima dashboard, export all subscriber data as a CSV file. It includes product names, customer emails and subscription dates for reporting, marketing or inventory planning.
