About Notifima

Notifima is a WooCommerce plugin that helps online stores recover sales from out-of-stock products. It lets customers join product waitlists and automatically sends back in stock emails when inventory is replenished, so you capture demand instead of losing customers.
Notifima replaces out-of-stock dead ends with a product waitlist. Customers subscribe to unavailable products and the plugin notifies them automatically when those products are back, improving retention and recovering missed sales.
WooCommerce store owners, eCommerce businesses, agencies and developers who want to reduce lost sales from stockouts. It suits stores of every size, from small shops to large online retailers.
To solve a common WooCommerce problem: customers leaving when products are out of stock. Instead of losing interested buyers, stores capture demand with waitlists and reconnect with shoppers automatically after restocking.
Install the plugin, enable product waitlists and let customers subscribe to out-of-stock items. When inventory is updated, Notifima sends back in stock notifications to subscribers, with very little manual effort after setup.
Yes. Notifima supports simple and variable products. Customers can subscribe to an individual variation, which makes notifications more relevant and improves the shopping experience.
