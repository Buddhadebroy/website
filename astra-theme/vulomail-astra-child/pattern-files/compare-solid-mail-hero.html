Comparison

VuloMail vs Solid Mail.

Solid Mail, formerly WP SMTP, is free and does the basics well: five API providers plus manual SMTP, multiple connections chosen by From address, a fallback, and an email log. It was last updated in May 2026 and is listed as tested up to WordPress 6.9.10, behind the current 7.1.3. VuloMail supports far more services and adds alerts, diagnostics and SMS.
