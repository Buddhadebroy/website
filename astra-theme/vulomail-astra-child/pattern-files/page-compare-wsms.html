Comparison

VuloMail vs WSMS (WP SMS).

WSMS, formerly WP SMS, is a full SMS toolkit: 200+ gateways in the free version, admin alerts, WooCommerce notifications, subscriber forms, and, on its paid plan, OTP and two-factor login, scheduled campaigns and a two-way inbox. It doesn't deliver email. VuloMail is an email delivery plugin with focused SMS alerts through four gateways. If SMS is your main channel, WSMS goes much further.

Strengths

Where each one wins.

We list theirs first. If one of these matters most to you, it should decide it.

Where WSMS (WP SMS) is stronger

200+ SMS gateways in the free version, against VuloMail's four, including many regional ones.

OTP and two-factor login, scheduled campaigns, abandoned-cart texts and a two-way inbox on the paid plan.

Subscriber forms and bulk SMS for marketing.

Where VuloMail is stronger

VuloMail also fixes your WordPress email: 22 email APIs, 18 SMTP presets or any SMTP server, a backup connection and a log of every message.

Email and SMS in one log and one dashboard, with a text alert when an email fails.

Free, with no paid plan.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Choose WSMS (WP SMS) if

SMS is a main channel for you: marketing, OTP logins or local gateways VuloMail doesn't support.

Choose VuloMail if

You need reliable WordPress email first, and want order and site alerts by text through Twilio, Vonage, Plivo or Clickatell.

Switching

Using them together.

01

Keep both if you like

WSMS doesn't deliver email, so it doesn't compete with VuloMail for your site's messages.

02

Avoid double texts

If both plugins send the same alert, such as new-order texts, switch it off in one of them.

03

Add your email provider

Connect SMTP or an email service in VuloMail, set it as primary and send a test.
