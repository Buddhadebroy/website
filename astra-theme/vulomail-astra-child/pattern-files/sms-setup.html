Setup

Three steps, about three minutes.

1 1

Connect a gateway

Settings → Connections → Add SMS connection. Your first SMS connection becomes primary straight away, since there's no built-in way to send texts to fall back on.

2 2

Set your phone number

Settings → SMS → Admin phone number, in international format such as +14155550123. Alerts for you go there.

3 3

Switch alerts on

VuloMail → SMS Alerts. Each alert's message box appears when it's on. Leave it empty for the default, or write your own.
