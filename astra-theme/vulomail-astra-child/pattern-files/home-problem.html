The quiet problem

WordPress email fails without making a sound.

Order confirmations, password resets and form notifications are some of the most important messages your site sends. By default they travel the least reliable route.

Your host 1

Limits or blocks mail

WordPress sends from your web server by default. Many hosts throttle it or switch it off entirely.

The inbox 2

Sends it to spam

Gmail and other mailbox providers distrust server mail without proper sender records.

Your customer 3

Never tells you

Nobody writes in to say their receipt didn't arrive. Failures stay silent until they cost you.
