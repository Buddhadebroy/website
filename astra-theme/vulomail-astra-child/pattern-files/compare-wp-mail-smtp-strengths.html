Strengths

Where each one wins.

We list theirs first. If one of these matters most to you, it should decide it.

Where WP Mail SMTP is stronger

Track record: 4 million active installs and 5,205 reviews averaging 4.8 out of 5. VuloMail was listed on 12 October 2026 and has no reviews yet.

Sign in with Google in the free version, so no Gmail password or app password is stored on your site. VuloMail's Gmail preset uses an app password.

Pro has features VuloMail doesn't offer at any price: open and click tracking, weekly email summaries, rate limiting, background sending queues, conditional routing and multisite network settings.

Paid one-to-one support and a White Glove Setup service that configures DNS and the mailer for you.

Where VuloMail is stronger

Email log, resend, backup connection and failure alerts are free in VuloMail. In WP Mail SMTP each needs Pro, $99 a year for one site at full price.

Amazon SES, Microsoft 365 and Zoho presets are in the free plugin, alongside 22 email APIs. WP Mail SMTP keeps all three for Pro.

SMS: WooCommerce order, stock and review alerts for you, and order updates for customers, through Twilio, Vonage, Plivo or Clickatell. WP Mail SMTP only texts you when an email fails, and only in Pro.

No outside service in the loop: VuloMail's DNS check runs from your own server, and it never contacts a VuloLabs server. WP Mail SMTP's domain check sends your From address and domain to its own service.
