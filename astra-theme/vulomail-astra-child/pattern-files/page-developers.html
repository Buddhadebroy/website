Make it opt-in

Installing VuloMail shouldn't silently change how your plugin delivers. Offer a setting, off by default for existing sites.

Keep your content

Templates, recipients and the decision to send stay in your plugin. VuloMail is only the transport.

One-way dependency

Your plugin calls VuloMail. VuloMail never calls into another plugin. WooCommerce alerts are the one exception.

Ship delivery you don't have to maintain.

Hand your email and SMS to VuloMail when it's present, and keep your own behaviour when it isn't.
