Strengths

Where each one wins.

We list theirs first. If one of these matters most to you, it should decide it.

Where FluentSMTP is stronger

Sign in with Google and Microsoft (OAuth), so no mailbox password is stored. VuloMail's Gmail and Microsoft presets use app passwords.

Failure alerts to Telegram, Slack, Discord and Pushover, plus a daily health check of every connection.

Route different From addresses to different providers, and manage it all from WP-CLI.

Option to keep credentials in wp-config.php instead of the database.

A five-year track record: 700,000 active installs and 407 reviews averaging 4.8 out of 5.

Where VuloMail is stronger

SMS is built in: 18 alerts for your site and WooCommerce store, including order updates texted to customers. FluentSMTP has no SMS at all.

More email services: 22 email APIs and 18 SMTP presets, including Mailjet, MailerSend, Mailtrap, Mandrill, Resend, SendLayer, ZeptoMail, SocketLabs and SendPulse, which FluentSMTP doesn't list.

Privacy defaults: message bodies aren't stored unless you opt in, recipients can be masked, and old entries prune themselves after 30 days.

If both connections fail, WordPress's own mailer can still try as a last resort, and every attempt appears on a single log row.

A sender-domain check for SPF and DMARC records in Diagnostics.
