Strengths

Where each one wins.

We list theirs first. If one of these matters most to you, it should decide it.

Where Post SMTP is stronger

A mobile app and Chrome extension for watching delivery away from WordPress.

Gmail through Google's API with OAuth in the free version; VuloMail's Gmail preset uses an app password.

A DKIM check alongside SPF and DMARC. VuloMail checks SPF and DMARC and leaves DKIM to your provider's dashboard.

Paid extras VuloMail doesn't have: open tracking, automatic retries, email batching and a weekly health report.

On WordPress.org since 2017, with 300,000 active installs and 528 reviews averaging 4.7 out of 5.

Where VuloMail is stronger

Microsoft 365 and Amazon SES work in the free plugin; Post SMTP needs a paid plan for both.

SMS alerts for your site and store, and order updates texted to customers, without a Twilio extension licence.

If both connections fail, WordPress's own mailer can still try as a last resort.

Privacy defaults: bodies aren't stored unless you opt in, recipients can be masked, and VuloMail contacts no vendor server.
