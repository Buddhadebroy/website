Providers

Use the account you already pay for.

22 email APIs, 18 one-click SMTP presets, any SMTP server and 4 SMS gateways, out of the box. VuloMail only contacts the providers you connect, and saved keys are encrypted the moment you press Save.

Connections

Primary, backup, and nothing confusing.

A connection is one account with a provider. Add as many as you like, then choose which one is primary and which is the backup. A connection can't be both.

Primary

In use for every message

Backup

Used when the primary fails

Disabled

Switched off in its Edit form; skipped

Incomplete

A required detail is missing or a saved key can't be read

Built in

The WordPress default mailer, when it isn't primary

Email waits for you

A new email connection is saved but not used until you click Set as primary. WordPress stays the default until then.

SMS starts right away

The first SMS connection becomes primary automatically, because there's no built-in SMS to fall back on.

Fixed once saved

A connection's provider can't change later, because its stored secrets belong to it. Add a new connection instead.

SMTP, in detail

Works with almost any mailbox.

Gmail, Microsoft 365, Zoho, Amazon SES and 14 more have presets: pick one and VuloMail fills in the host, port and encryption, then asks only for your address and password. Most need an app password rather than your normal one. Anything else connects through plain SMTP, usually port 587 with STARTTLS or 465 with SSL/TLS.

Your provider, your rules.

Missing a provider? Any developer can add one with a single PHP class. The connection form builds itself.
