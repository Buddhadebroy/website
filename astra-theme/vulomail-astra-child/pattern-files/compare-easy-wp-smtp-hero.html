Comparison

VuloMail vs Easy WP SMTP.

Easy WP SMTP has 500,000 installs and a 4.6-star rating. The free version sends through nine services or any SMTP server and records failed sends as debug events. The full email log, backup connection, alerts, and Gmail, Microsoft 365 or Amazon SES sending are all paid, from $99 a year at full price. VuloMail includes those for free and adds SMS, but it has no track record yet.
