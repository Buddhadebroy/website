Comparison

VuloMail vs Easy WP SMTP.

Easy WP SMTP has 500,000 installs and a 4.6-star rating. The free version sends through nine services or any SMTP server and records failed sends as debug events. The full email log, backup connection, alerts, and Gmail, Microsoft 365 or Amazon SES sending are all paid, from $99 a year at full price. VuloMail includes those for free and adds SMS, but it has no track record yet.

Strengths

Where each one wins.

We list theirs first. If one of these matters most to you, it should decide it.

Where Easy WP SMTP is stronger

Track record: on WordPress.org since 2013, with 500,000 active installs and 708 reviews averaging 4.6 out of 5.

Paid plans add features VuloMail doesn't have: one-click Google sign-in, open and click tracking, email reports, rate limiting, background sending and conditional routing.

Paid plans include vendor support; Elite adds priority support.

Where VuloMail is stronger

A full email log, resend, backup connection and failure alerts are free in VuloMail. Easy WP SMTP charges $99 a year for them on one site.

Gmail, Microsoft 365 and Amazon SES all work in the free plugin. Easy WP SMTP puts all three behind a paid plan.

SMS alerts for your site and WooCommerce store, and order-update texts to customers.

A sender-domain SPF and DMARC check in Diagnostics.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Choose Easy WP SMTP if

You want a long-established plugin with paid vendor support.

You'll pay for open and click tracking, email reports or rate limiting.

Choose VuloMail if

You want logs, backup and alerts without paying every year.

You send through Gmail, Microsoft 365 or Amazon SES and don't want a licence just for that.

You run a WooCommerce store and want SMS alerts too.
