SMS alerts

A text reaches you when email doesn't.

Even when your site's email itself has stopped working. Connect your own SMS gateway, then choose from 18 alerts for your site, your store and your customers.

Every alert

Pick an alert. Write the message.

Switch alerts on and edit their templates below. Placeholders fill in with sample values so you can see the real text, and the segment count tells you what you'll be billed.

Store and customer alerts need WooCommerce. Without it they're shown but locked. Only switch on customer alerts if your customers have agreed to receive texts. The rules differ by country, and you're responsible for following them.

Setup

Three steps, about three minutes.

1 1

Connect a gateway

Settings → Connections → Add SMS connection. Your first SMS connection becomes primary straight away, since there's no built-in way to send texts to fall back on.

2 2

Set your phone number

Settings → SMS → Admin phone number, in international format such as +14155550123. Alerts for you go there.

3 3

Switch alerts on

VuloMail → SMS Alerts. Each alert's message box appears when it's on. Leave it empty for the default, or write your own.

The details

Small rules that save real money.

Phone numbers, normalised

Numbers starting with + or 00 are international. Others get your default country code, with leading zeros dropped. Without a country code, a national number is refused rather than guessed.

Segments you can predict

160 plain characters per text, or 70 with emoji and non-Latin scripts. Longer texts are split and billed as several. 1,530 characters is the hard ceiling.

One text per order change

Processing, completed, refunded and cancelled each have their own customer alert. Any other status falls through to the general alert, never both.

Built-in restraint

Admin-login alerts only fire for administrators. Spam comments are ignored. The email-failed alert sends at most one text every 15 minutes and ignores test emails.
