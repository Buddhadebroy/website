VuloMail vs Site Mailer.

Site Mailer (now called Email Deliverability) works differently from every other plugin here: instead of connecting your own email provider, it sends through Elementor's service and needs an Elementor account. The free plan covers 200 emails in total, not per month. It is rated 2.5 out of 5 from 17 reviews. VuloMail is a free connector for a provider you choose and pay directly.
