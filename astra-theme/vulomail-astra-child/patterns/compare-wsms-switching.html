Switching

Using them together.

01

Keep both if you like

WSMS doesn't deliver email, so it doesn't compete with VuloMail for your site's messages.

02

Avoid double texts

If both plugins send the same alert, such as new-order texts, switch it off in one of them.

03

Add your email provider

Connect SMTP or an email service in VuloMail, set it as primary and send a test.
