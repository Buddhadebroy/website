Security & privacy

Your keys stay locked. Your log stays home.

VuloMail handles the credentials for your email and SMS accounts and a record of what your site sends. Here's exactly how it treats both.

Encrypted at rest

Every password and API key is encrypted with libsodium's secretbox and a random nonce before it touches the database.

Never shown again

Saved secrets come back as eight dots and the last four characters. Leave a masked field alone and the stored value is kept.

HTTPS only, no redirects

Outbound calls refuse anything but https, never follow a redirect that could forward your key, and refuse private and loopback addresses.

Scrubbed error text

Provider responses can echo a credential. Known secrets, Authorization headers and key=value pairs are removed before an error is logged or shown.

Injection guards

No line breaks are allowed in any stored value, address, name or header. Account IDs are validated before they enter a URL.

Administrators only

Every screen and every REST route requires manage_options. Logged bodies are shown as plain text, never rendered as HTML.

Split-key encryption

Two halves. Neither is enough alone.

The encryption key is derived from a secret in your site's config and random material in your database. Someone holding a database dump can't read your credentials, and neither can someone holding only wp-config.php.

The trade-off, stated plainly: if your site's salts change, for example after a migration or a security clean-up, saved credentials can no longer be read. The connection shows Incomplete and Diagnostics flags it; enter the key again. Define VULOMAIL_ENCRYPTION_KEY to control this yourself.

Privacy

Strict by default. Generous when you choose.

Message content

Off by default

Bodies can contain password-reset links and personal details. Store them only if you need to read or resend messages.

Masked recipients

Optional

Store j•••@example.com and •••••••••123 instead of full addresses and numbers. Masked entries can't be resent.

Retention

30 days

Older entries are pruned daily. Set 0 to keep everything, or up to 3,650 days.

Logging

On

Turn it off entirely if you must not keep any record. The dashboard totals then stay at zero.

No post types, no user meta, no post meta. On uninstall, nothing is removed unless you chose Delete everything. Then the table, options, transients and scheduled event are all cleared.

No tracking. No phoning home.

VuloMail contacts only the providers you connect, and only to send the messages your site asks it to. It contacts no VuloLabs server and needs no account.
