Features

Small plugin. Serious delivery.

Everything VuloMail does, on one page. Routing and failover, a delivery log you can actually read, SMS alerts, diagnostics, and security and privacy rules that are strict by default.

01

Routing & failover.

Choose a primary connection and, optionally, a backup. Every email your site sends through wp_mail() takes that route. If both fail, WordPress's own mailer can still try as a last resort.

Primary and backup slots for email and for SMS

Failover on any failure, including a permanent rejection

Optional last resort: hand the message to WordPress

Nothing changes until you set a primary

Respects plugins that deliberately short-circuit wp_mail()

Delete a primary and the backup is promoted

02

A log you can read.

One row per message, however many connections were tried. Each entry shows who it was for, which provider sent it, the plugin or theme that created it, and the reason a failure happened, in the provider's own words.

Filter by channel, status and date range; search recipients and subjects

Status pills that count within your current filters

Every attempt listed in order, with the provider's message ID

Resend through your current routing (when content was stored)

Dates in your site's own format, timezone and language

Delete one entry, or clear the whole log

03

The dashboard puts failures first.

Email and SMS fail quietly. A customer won't tell you their receipt never came. The dashboard answers three questions at a glance: how much did my site send, did anything fail, and what is each channel sending through.

Sent and failed totals for 7, 30 or 90 days

Recent failures grouped by day, with the provider's reason

Channel status: Active, Not set up, or Switched off

Notices when email still uses WordPress's mailer or logging is off

04

SMS through your own gateway.

Connect Twilio, Vonage, Plivo or Clickatell and switch on the alerts you want: for you, about your site and store, or for your customers, about their orders.

18 alerts across site, store and customer events

Every alert off until you switch it on

Editable templates with {placeholders}

International numbers, or national ones with a default country code

One text per order change, never two

Other plugins can hand their SMS to VuloMail

05

Test it before a customer does.

Send a test email or SMS through live routing, the same path every other message takes, or through one named connection to check a backup you rarely use. Diagnostics then looks for the usual causes of email not arriving.

Test through live routing or one specific connection

Every attempt shown when a test fails

11 read-only checks: never changes a setting or sends anything

SPF and DMARC lookups for your sender domain

Spots other active email plugins competing for wp_mail()

A “What to do” and a button to the right screen

06

Security that assumes the worst.

Credentials are encrypted at rest, masked on screen and never sent back to the browser. Outbound requests are locked to HTTPS with no redirects, and error text is scrubbed before it reaches your log.

libsodium secretbox encryption, split-key design

Masked secrets: eight dots and the last four characters

HTTPS only, no redirects, private addresses refused

Account IDs validated before they enter a URL

Header-injection guards on every address and header

Administrators only, on every screen and API route

07

Privacy by default.

Message bodies can hold password-reset links and personal details, so they're not stored unless you ask. The log lives in your own database and never leaves your site.

Message content off by default

Mask recipients as j•••@example.com

Automatic pruning after 30 days (or your choice, up to 10 years)

Keep data or delete everything on uninstall

No tracking, no VuloLabs server, no account

Data only goes to the providers you connect

08

An admin that stays out of the way.

One focused screen inside WordPress with Dashboard, Logs, Tools, SMS Alerts and Settings. Changes save themselves. A search box finds any tab, setting or section by name.

Autosave: no Save button to forget

Header search across every setting

Deep links straight to a log entry or test

Connection forms generated from each provider's definition

WooCommerce features clearly locked when it isn't active

Translation-ready

Make every message count.

Install VuloMail, connect the provider you already pay for, and send your first test in about five minutes.
