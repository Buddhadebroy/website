Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Choose WP Mail SMTP if

You want the most proven plugin and the largest support community.

You need open and click tracking, weekly reports, rate limiting or multisite network controls, and you're happy to pay for Pro.

You send through Gmail and want Google sign-in instead of an app password.

Choose VuloMail if

You want a log, a backup connection and failure alerts without a yearly licence.

You run WooCommerce and want texts about orders and stock, or order updates sent to customers.

You use one of the less common services VuloMail supports, such as ZeptoMail, SocketLabs, Mailtrap or Bird.
