Find

Find your niche

Pick a niche you already belong to. It should pass all three tests.

PassionPainPurchasing
PowerProfit

Passion test

Do they join groups, follow creators, or tattoo it? Then it's a niche worth chasing.

Pain test

What do they complain they can't find? That's your gap.

Purchasing power test

Do they happily spend $500+ on it? Then it can pay.

Building a community, not just a platform

The community-first mindset.

  • Facilitate connections between members
  • Celebrate successes publicly
  • Address conflicts quickly and fairly
  • Create traditions and recurring events
Discord / SlackChannels, weekly AMAs, early accessUser-generated contentPhoto contests, challenges, storiesOffline to onlineMeetups, trade shows, pop-ups
