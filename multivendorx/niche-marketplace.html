Chapter 2 · Find

Finding Your Golden Niche (the $10M question)

Not all niches are created equal. The best ones sit at the intersection of three things.

PassionPainPurchasing
PowerProfit

Passion test

Do they join groups, follow creators, or tattoo it? Then it's a niche worth chasing.

Pain test

What do they vent about on Reddit, rate 1-star, or call "impossible to find"? That's your gap to fill.

Purchasing power test

Do they spend $500+, buy premium basics, and treat it as an investment? That's a profitable niche.

Chapter 3 · Find

The 72-Hour Validation Sprint

Spend three days proving people care before you spend three months building something they don't want.

Day 0-1

Research reconnaissance

Join 5 communities (Facebook, Reddit, Discord), screenshot 20 pain points and note recurring complaints.

Day 1-2

Validation landing page

"The [Niche] Marketplace You've Been Waiting For". Solve their #1 pain, grab emails with a lead magnet, share in communities.

Day 2-3

Direct outreach

Reach out to 50 niche sellers, ask their top challenge selling online, mention your solution and log interest.

After 72 hours, check your resultsUse these numbers to decide whether to build or rethink your niche.
What to measure Build it Rethink it
Email signups100+ signups in 72 hoursFewer than 20 signups
Seller outreach replies30%+ response rateUnder 10% response rate
How people respondPeople asking "when will this be ready?"Generic, unenthusiastic responses
Seller interestSellers offering to be beta testersNo one asking follow-up questions
