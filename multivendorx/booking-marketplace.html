Grow

Repeat bookings, not one-off visits

The money is in the customer who books every month.

  • Send reminders at 24 h and 2 h before every booking
  • Make rebooking one click from the confirmation
  • Show only reviews from real bookings
  • Handle cancellations and refunds quickly and fairly
Local searchGoogle Business Profile, one "[service] in [city]" page a weekPartnersGyms, offices and shops that send customers your wayReferralsRewards for both the customer and the friend
