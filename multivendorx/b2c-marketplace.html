§01 · Find

The B2B Opportunity (why business buyers are different)

Business buying works differently from shopping. Three things make it a strong marketplace model, and focused players keep proving that niches win.

01Recurring demandBusinesses buy the same things again and again, on a schedule.
02Less price-sensitiveBuyers weigh reliability, quality and return on investment over the lowest price.
03Long relationshipsA buyer who trusts a supplier stays for years, not one order.

Focus wins: what niche marketplaces got right

StockX

One category, sneakers, with authentication buyers could trust.

Etsy

Reverb

Musical instruments, built by people who play them.

Warby Parker

Solved trust with home try-ons before asking anyone to pay.

§03 · Find

The Research Sprint

B2B buyers won't sign up for a teaser. Talk to them instead. Your task: complete the three research tracks below, then use the table to decide: build, narrow or switch.

Track 1

Buyer interviews

Interview 20+ buyers for 15 minutes each and survey 100+ more. Ask how they source, reorder and pay today.

Track 2

Supplier interviews

Interview 15+ suppliers. Map how they sell now and what frustrates them about existing platforms.

Track 3

Listen in

Spend two weeks in industry groups, newsletters and forums. Note the problems that come up again and again.

After the sprint, check your resultsA "rethink" costs you a week or two, not a year: narrow the industry or the product range and research again.

Metrics and a monthly rhythm
