A renter who comes back every season is worth more than ten who rent once.

  • Take condition photos at pickup and return
  • Settle damage quickly and fairly
  • Put a "rent again" link in every follow-up email

Is this for you?

Renting out things, not time? Then this playbook is for you.

A good fit

  • Pricey items people use now and then: cameras, tools, party gear, outdoor kit
  • Many owners, each listing their own items
  • You want a cut of every rental, not to own the stock

Not the right fit

  • You own all the stock yourself. A rental plugin alone will do.
  • Services by the session. Read the booking playbook.
  • Overnight stays. MultiVendorX's accommodation features suit you better.
