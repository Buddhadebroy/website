Frequently asked questions

What is MultiVendorX?

MultiVendorX is a powerful WordPress plugin that transforms your WooCommerce store into a full-featured multi-vendor marketplace, allowing multiple vendors to sell products and manage their own stores.

Can I create a multi-vendor marketplace with WooCommerce?

Yes. MultiVendorX extends WooCommerce so you can onboard unlimited vendors, create independent storefronts, automate commissions and payouts, and support physical, digital, booking, rental or subscription products.

Does MultiVendorX support Stripe Connect split payments?

Yes. Stripe Connect split payments enable automatic commission distribution between admins and vendors, with flexible commission structures, payout schedules and transaction fees.

Does it support PayPal Marketplace payouts?

Yes. The PayPal Marketplace integration handles vendor payouts, commission management and payment distribution across multiple vendors.

Can multiple vendors sell the same product?

Yes. Shared Listings let multiple vendors sell the same product with different pricing, stock levels, shipping rules and storefront policies.

Can admins set different commissions per vendor or product?

Yes. Configure percentage, fixed, hybrid or tiered commissions based on vendor plans, memberships, product categories, regions or subscription levels.

Is MultiVendorX scalable for enterprise marketplaces?

Yes. It supports large vendor catalogs, distributed storefronts, regional operations and advanced workflows via Shared Listings, Franchise, Staff Manager, Wholesale and more.
