02

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss a potential project.
  • To scope, deliver, and support engagements you've contracted us for.
  • To improve this website and understand how it's used.
  • To meet legal, accounting and security obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use project or contact data for purposes unrelated to the engagement without asking first.

03

Sharing your information

We share information only where it's needed to run our business or deliver your project — for example, with the cloud infrastructure, analytics, email, or payment providers that support our website and services. Any third party we share data with is required to protect it and use it only for the purpose we've engaged them for. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

04

Cookies

This site may use essential cookies to make the site function correctly, and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use it. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect parts of the site's functionality.

05

Data retention

We keep contact and project data for as long as it's needed to deliver our services, maintain business records, and meet legal or contractual obligations — after which it's deleted or anonymised.

06

Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

07

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to minimise risk and respond promptly to any incident.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. The version published on this page is the one currently in effect.
