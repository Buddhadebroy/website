§ 01 The real problem

Software rarely fails in the code. It fails in the understanding.

Wrong requirements, over-engineering, fragile integrations, and an MVP that never ends. Most of these failures are baked in before anyone writes a line of code. So we work differently.

The usual wayThe Vulo Labs way

Write the spec, then hand it to developers.

→

Understand the job first. The spec comes out of that.

Start with the stack everyone's talking about.

→

Pick the simplest stack that survives the next stage.

Ship every feature in version one.

→

Ship the smallest thing that proves the idea, then grow it.

Design is the coat of paint at the end.

→

Design decides whether anyone uses it. It starts on day one.

§ 04 Process

A partner, not a vendor.

Six stages, fully visible. Join at whichever one you're at. Plenty of projects start halfway down this list.

Start at stage one ↗
  1. stage_01

    Discover

    We learn the business before the code: goals, users, workflows, constraints, and where the MVP ends.

  2. stage_02

    Architect

    Requirements become a blueprint covering data, user flows, APIs, security and how it scales.

  3. stage_03

    Build

    Short iterations you can see and click. No disappearing for three months.

  4. stage_04

    Test

    Functionality, performance, integrations, edge cases and security, in production-like conditions.

  5. stage_05

    Launch

    Deployment, analytics, monitoring, documentation and someone on hand on launch day.

  6. stage_06 ↺

    Improve

    Launch is when the learning starts: optimise, add features, automate, scale.

§ 05 House rules

Software should make your business easier to run. These rules keep us honest.

1

Problem before product.

We ask why it should exist before we decide how to build it.

2

Boring tech, on purpose.

Not every startup needs microservices. We choose for your stage, not for our CV.

3

Version one is not the last version.

Everything is built expecting change.

4

Business outcomes over tickets.

Revenue, efficiency, customer experience and scale are the real spec.

5

One team, start to finish.

Design, engineering, DevOps and QA in one place. No relay race.

§ 08 FAQ

Asked before every build.

Something else? hello@vulolabs.com goes straight to an engineer.

Q01Is Vulo Labs just a SaaS shop?

No. We build SaaS, WordPress platforms, web apps, custom business software, AI products, integrations and internal tools.

Q02I only have an idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Most good projects start there. We help shape the product, scope the MVP and turn it into a technical plan before anything is built.

Q03Can you take over an existing product?

Yes: new features, performance work, bug fixing, architecture clean-up, integrations, AI features or ongoing maintenance.

Q04Do you still do WordPress?

Very much. Custom sites, complex platforms, WooCommerce, custom plugins, integrations and long-term optimisation.

Q05Will you work with our developers, or behind our agency?

Both. We can join your team as an extension, or deliver white-label behind an agency.

Q06When should we bring you in?

Earlier than you think. Product and architecture decisions are cheapest to change before they're built.

Q07What happens after launch?

We can stay on for maintenance, new features, optimisation or as a dedicated engineering team.
