Our story

Built by the team behind MultiVendorX

CatalogX is a subsidiary of MultiVendorX. Building tools for WooCommerce marketplaces, we kept seeing the same gap: B2B sellers, wholesalers and makers of custom products were forcing buyers through a checkout that didn't fit how they buy.

So we built one plugin that covers the whole journey, from a catalog without prices to a signed-off quote that becomes an order, with wholesale pricing and role-based rules in the same place.
