Questions about quotes

How request-a-quote works in WooCommerce with CatalogX, and how it compares to other options.

Contact support {i:arrow}
With CatalogX, customers request a quote in three steps:
  1. They open any product page and click the "Request a Quote" button (instead of Add to Cart).
  2. They fill out a customizable form with their name, email, quantity needed, and any special requirements.
  3. They submit the request. It lands in your CatalogX Enquiry Portal, and they receive a confirmation email.
Unlike traditional WooCommerce, this replaces the checkout entirely for B2B buyers. They never see a price tag or cart: they submit their request, you review it, and you respond with a custom quote via email or PDF.
Both let you add a request a quote button, but CatalogX solves the full B2B problem in one plugin: request a quote, quotation management, wholesale pricing tiers, dynamic role-based pricing, and one-click quote-to-order conversion, all from one dashboard with no separate extensions. YITH Request a Quote focuses on the quote request part. For wholesale pricing you need a separate plugin, and for dynamic pricing another extension. For B2B stores, CatalogX is cheaper (one plugin fee vs. three) and faster to set up (one settings page vs. managing multiple extensions).

With CatalogX you can hide prices site-wide for all visitors, hide prices only for guest users while showing them to logged-in wholesale buyers, hide prices for specific categories or individual products, and replace the Add to Cart button with "Request a Quote", "Get Pricing", or custom text you choose. You control exactly who sees what price (or no price) and which button they see instead.
  1. Customer requests a quote on one or more products.
  2. You review the request in the CatalogX Enquiry Portal.
  3. You create a custom quote with your pricing, shipping, and terms.
  4. You send the quote as a PDF or email link.
  5. Customer clicks "Accept & Checkout" in the email or PDF.
  6. The quote converts into a WooCommerce order automatically, with no re-entry and no re-negotiation.
  7. The payment link is sent, the order is placed, and you fulfil it.
The entire flow takes about 30 seconds per quote. No manual order entry, no missed communication.
Yes, the Wholesale Module is built in. You can set different prices for wholesale, retail, VIP, and guest user roles; create bulk quantity discounts (for example, 50+ units = 20% off); apply role-based pricing to wholesale customers automatically; show wholesale prices only to logged-in wholesale buyers; and let wholesale customers add items to a quote cart and request quotes at their negotiated rates. Wholesale buyers see their prices and retail buyers see theirs, all from one product page.
Customers can download and view quotes as PDFs once you send them:
  1. You create the quote with pricing and terms in your admin panel.
  2. CatalogX generates a professional PDF with your logo, branding, and terms.
  3. The PDF is attached to the email or available in the customer's account.
  4. The customer can download, review, share internally, and accept from the PDF.
Customers cannot generate their own quotes. You control the pricing and terms, which keeps B2B negotiation professional and prevents quote spam.
CatalogX supports a "customer offered price" field where buyers can suggest the price they're willing to pay. You see it in the Enquiry Portal, review it, and decide whether to accept, counter-offer, or decline. This is powerful for negotiation-heavy industries like manufacturing, wholesale, and industrial supply.
