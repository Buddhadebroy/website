Community FAQ

About the community

Who it's for and how to take part.

The CatalogX Community is a collaborative platform where WooCommerce merchants, developers, and eCommerce professionals connect to exchange ideas, share experiences, report issues, suggest features, and contribute to improving CatalogX products.
Anyone using CatalogX products can join, including WooCommerce store owners, developers, agencies, consultants, and businesses looking to learn from others and influence future product development.
Joining gives you direct access to product discussions, feature requests, expert advice, troubleshooting help, and opportunities to collaborate with other WooCommerce professionals.
Yes. Community members are encouraged to submit feature requests, discuss improvements, and vote on ideas that help shape future CatalogX releases.
