Strong opinions, with the reasoning attached.

Disagree? Good. Tell me why.

  1. On entrepreneurship

    Distribution beats features.

    Products rarely fail for lack of one more feature. They fail because the right customer never finds them. Before I build the next module, I work out how the first thousand users will arrive.

  2. On product

    A great product is not yet a business.

    A product solves a problem. A business also needs pricing that holds up, customers who stay, and a repeatable way to reach new ones. I treat those as product work, not afterthoughts.

  3. On AI

    AI is changing the economics of software.

    Work that once took a team a quarter can increasingly be done in days. When building gets cheaper, the advantage moves to knowing the customer and reaching them.

  4. On experience

    Fewer features, clearer experience.

    Every extra option is a decision handed to the customer. The products people stick with make the first five minutes obvious and keep the rest out of the way until it is needed.

  5. On open source

    Free can be a business, if trust comes first.

    A free product earns attention and trust. Revenue follows only when the paid offer solves a bigger problem for the people already using the free one.
