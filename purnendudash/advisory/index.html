How it works

This is for you if the following sounds familiar.

  • Founders with a product and no technical leader

    The idea works, customers are arriving, and every technical decision is being made by whoever is available.

  • Marketplace and learning businesses outgrowing the first build

    Commissions, payouts, enrollment, and reporting start to creak once real volume arrives.

  • Teams on WordPress or WooCommerce that need real architecture

    A plugin pile that works today but cannot take the next feature without breaking something.

  • Companies adding AI without a clear plan

    Interest is high and direction is thin. Someone has to say where it helps and where it does not.

Six areas where senior judgment pays off.

  1. Technical strategy

    Decide what to build, what to buy, and what to leave alone, tied to the business plan rather than the latest tool.

  2. Architecture

    Data models, extension points, and the logic for commissions or enrollment, designed so the product can grow without rewrites.

  3. Product and roadmap

    Turn customer problems into a sequenced roadmap the team can actually ship.

  4. Team and process

    Hiring bar, code review, release process, and how engineers and product people work together.

  5. Reliability and upkeep

    Compatibility, security updates, support load, and the long-term maintenance a plugin or open source product needs.

  6. AI adoption

    Where AI lowers the cost of building or serving customers, and where it adds risk for no gain.

Four steps, from first call to handover.

  1. step 1

    Diagnose

    Review the product, the code, the team, and the goals. Find the few problems that actually matter.

  2. step 2

    Plan

    A short written plan: priorities, architecture decisions, and the risks worth watching.

  3. step 3

    Embed

    Work alongside the team every week: decisions, reviews, hiring, and clearing blockers.

  4. step 4

    Hand over

    Leave documented decisions and a team that can run without outside leadership, or continue for as long as it helps.

Time commitment, focus, and length are agreed per company after the first conversation. There are no fixed packages.
